# Defect Management

**Status:** Approved

## Severidade

Critical / High / Medium / Low, baseada em impacto e risco.

## Critical

Security/tenant leak, data corruption, inability to publish/restore
governance state ou severe availability issue.

## Registro

Steps, expected/actual, environment, evidence, correlation IDs e
affected scope.

## Regression

Defeito corrigido deve gerar teste quando economicamente razoável.
