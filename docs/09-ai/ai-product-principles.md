# AI Product Principles

**Status:** Approved

1.  Assist, do not silently govern.
2.  Evidence before confidence.
3.  Facts, inference and recommendation must be distinguishable.
4.  Human confirmation for consequential changes.
5.  AI never expands authorization.
6.  Explainability over opaque automation.
7.  Provider independence through gateway/adapters.
8.  Cost-aware model selection.
9.  Graceful degradation.
10. Audit consequential AI-assisted actions.

## Value

A IA deve reduzir tempo de análise, preparação de status/comitês e busca
de contexto, sem enfraquecer governança.
