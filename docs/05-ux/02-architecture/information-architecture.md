# Information Architecture

**Status:** Approved

## Hierarquia

``` text
Platform
└── Tenant
    ├── Portfolio
    │   └── Project
    │       ├── Execution
    │       ├── Governance
    │       ├── Status
    │       └── History
    ├── Reports
    ├── Copilot
    └── Settings
```

## Áreas

1.  Daily Governance --- Meu Dia.
2.  Executive Governance --- Executive Portfolio.
3.  Portfolio & Projects.
4.  Execution --- Milestones, Deliverables.
5.  Governance --- Risks, Issues, Actions, Decisions, Dependencies.
6.  Control --- Status, RAG, Financial, Resources.
7.  Knowledge & History --- Documents, Time Machine, Reports.
8.  Intelligence --- PMO Copilot.
9.  Administration --- Settings.

## Princípio

A IA e dashboards atravessam os módulos, mas não substituem entidades
operacionais.
